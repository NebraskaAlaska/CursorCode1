# Independent evidence for scandium leaching from red mud in citric acid

**22 November 2026.** Can a model predict how much scandium an independent laboratory measures in solution after a two-hour leach of untreated red mud with pure aqueous citric acid? The sources screened so far yielded **zero qualifying test lots**. The variable-loading model has not been fitted or scored. Its accuracy on a new lot remains unknown.

## Development evidence

Gentzmann’s Hungarian residue provides 27 measured recoveries at 18 distinct combinations of temperature, acid strength and liquid volume per gram of dry solid. Supplementary Table A5 specifies a two-hour design. Ten A5 run IDs match Table B1 directly. B1 does not restate time for every row. Four additional confirmation measurements repeat one setting in the same laboratory. Ray’s Indian data belong to a separate, fixed-loading model at 50 mL/g, outside the Hungarian range of 5–23.41 mL/g. [Gentzmann study](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gexplo.2022.107041) · [Tables A5, B1 and B3](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0375674222000991-mmc1.docx) · [Ray study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-17134-7)

## The 14 Byers candidates

The version 2 Byers index contained **305 distinct study IDs** across 388 CSV rows. Screening citation, material and method fields identified 13 studies classified as red mud and one on slag made from red mud. Concentration and extraction-yield columns were excluded from selection. The groupings below account for all 14 studies. [Byers index](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/y9dbgc6th7/2)

- **Citric acid, different duration (1).** Lallemand’s ligand experiments used 48-hour leaching rounds. [Lallemand](https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.1c08081)
- **Other organic acids (2).** Kozyrev used formic acid followed by water through a column. Vielma used sequential extractions, including a 16-hour acetic-acid step. [Kozyrev](https://rudmet.net/media/articles/Article_NFM_02_22_pp.30-34.pdf) · [Vielma](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/17/5/1849)
- **Biological treatments (3).** Zhang used pyrite and elemental sulfur in two biological stages. Ilkhani used a four-hour oxalic-acid pretreatment. Kiskira studied fungal leaching. None established a separate series of three qualifying citrate-only controls. [Zhang](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cej.2020.125914) · [Ilkhani](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mineng.2024.108603) · [Kiskira](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12649-023-02116-5)
- **Different leaching reagents (3).** Daminescu measured filtrate Sc using mineral acids. Tsakanika studied phosphoric acid and compared inorganic acid treatments. Li (2022) used oxalic and sulfuric acids. [Daminescu](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/16/21/6998) · [Tsakanika](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-4701/12/2/228) · [Li, 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2213343722015238)
- **Processed solids or different extraction sequence (5).** Li (2025) roasted with iron sulfate before water leaching. Zinoveev used HCl on tailings after iron removal. Shoppert (2022) studied residue from alkali fusion and leaching. Archambo tested iron-nugget slag. Shoppert (2024) used reductive treatment followed by sulfuric acid extraction. ## Other sources and remaining gaps

- **Li’s separate 2026 Zenodo workbook.** Two liquid worksheets contain 30 and 28 numerical Sc entries in ppb. Their captions describe pH-controlled precipitation. They do not specify the citrate strength, temperature, time or liquid loading of preceding raw-residue leaches. The screen used headings, captions, cell types and counts without displaying the numerical Sc values. [Li workbook](https://zenodo.org/records/22986614)
- **Published experiments with a documented mismatch.** Shalchian reports one two-hour citrate condition with no separate filtrate Sc result. Dramou’s untreated-residue citrate runs lasted 60 minutes. Ujaczki’s acid comparison lasted 24 hours. Mishra tested six citrate strengths, but the elemental measurement was of an acid digest of treated solids and the reported analytes omitted Sc. [Shalchian](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/14/10/1044) · [Dramou, chapter 6](https://docnum.univ-lorraine.fr/public/DDOC_T_2024_0147_DRAMOU.pdf) · [Ujaczki](https://doi.org/10.1002/jctb.5300) · [Mishra](https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/%28ASCE%29HZ.2153-5515.0000541)
- **Unresolved.** Shan reports citrate extraction of scandium, but the recipe table and liquid-to-solid-ratio figure could not be verified. An optimum in a public abstract cannot establish three eligible runs. Some published optima were already visible during discovery. Any comparison using previously seen outcomes must be labelled retrospective. The regional archives, author records and forward citations screened do not exhaust all laboratory records. [Shan](https://doi.org/10.11785/S1000-4343.20240611)

## What would qualify for a blind comparison?

1. Identify one untreated lot from a different lab and at least three **distinct** 120-minute recipes using citric acid alone. Verify the acid preparation and concentration basis, temperature and starting liquid volume per gram of dry solid. Check each combination against the joint Hungarian design. Its separate factor ranges are 25–85 °C, 0.32–3.68 N and 5–23.41 mL/g. Converting from molarity requires a documented acid-equivalent convention.
2. Link each recipe to a measured dry-feed Sc assay and a separate filtered-liquid Sc sample, with units, volumes, aliquots or losses, dilution and analytical uncertainty. Use the recipe and sample IDs to reserve the entire external lot and every eligible row before development. Keep numerical test outcomes hidden.
3. Fit with development data alone, then freeze the model, its predictions and an equal-recipe mean-recovery baseline. Score all reserved rows once. Require mean absolute relative concentration error ≤20%, mean absolute recovery error ≤10 percentage points, worst absolute recovery error ≤15 points, and lower mean absolute error than the baseline for both outputs. A physical failure or uncertainty large enough to reverse the verdict precludes a pass. Any passing claim applies only to the tested material and conditions.
